## Why the council landed here

- Dream11 proved paid entry, which is exactly what is now banned; it is not evidence for a free version (Kunal, Rivers and dams; Nithin, Do unto customers).
- A free copy of free apps is about a 1-point gap, so the switch would not stick (Kunal, Delta 4).
- Ad money from Tier-2 students is too thin to carry a team; sponsors are the only real money, and none has signed (Deepinder, per-order rupee walk; Vembu, Spend-ratio red flag).
- Behaviour beats stated intent: a slide measures politeness, a no-code league measures return (Nithin and Vembu, against Kunal's first plan; Kunal conceded).

## Where they fought

- **B or C.** Kunal against Nithin, Deepinder and Vembu. Resolved: all four now want zero code until a no-code league and sponsor letters pass. Deepinder and Vembu dropped their build budgets; Kunal dropped his slide test.
- **What pays.** Deepinder modelled ads at Rs 60 a user a year (assumed); Vembu, Nithin and Kunal rejected ads. Resolved: sponsors only.
- **Days without a match.** Unresolved. No seat has evidence. Three seats suggest a season-long college table as the one thing to test.

## Minority report

Vembu scores B and C level at 5: "No code until 3 sponsors say yes in writing." His case is that a campus-league tool could pay its bills from local sponsors. For him to be right, three sponsors must sign within 90 days.

## The reframed version (Option B)

- **Cut:** entry fees, cash or cash-convertible prizes, display ads, the ad-free pass, and any app build before the gates.
- **First 10 customers:** students in 3 Tier-2 colleges outside the founder's friend circle, through one rooted student anchor per campus.
- **How money comes in:** brand-sponsored college trophies, paid upfront by the sponsor; no money from students.

## Conditions

1. Lawyer's written opinion on B (age gate, no cash-equivalent prizes) before collecting any student data.
2. At least 40% of league players return for match two with no prize, in at least one college.
3. At least 2 sponsors commit in writing (Vembu asks for 3) within 90 days.
4. Spend stays under Rs 2 lakh until 1 to 3 clear.

## What to do next

1. This week: run one 3-college league on WhatsApp and a spreadsheet for the next match series.
2. Within 30 days: pitch 10 local brands a Rs 25,000 college trophy sponsorship (assumed price).
3. Decision date: end of the first tournament. Gates met: reopen B with the council. Not met: C.

**7-day jugaad test:** one WhatsApp group per college, a Google Sheet scoreboard, and a free team-pick form for the next match. Cost under Rs 5,000. Pass mark: 40% of players submit a team for match two without being chased.
**Kill criteria:** under 40% return for match two, or zero paid sponsor interest after 10 pitches.
**Check before you build:** online money game ban and its final rules, children's data and ad targeting, sponsor disclosure. Verify with a lawyer before you build.
**Revisit:** after the first tournament, with return rates per college and sponsor letters.
