# Brief: Noida tiffin subscription

**Idea:** Home-style tiffin subscription for office workers in Noida Sector 62. Three home cooks, 60 meals/day, WhatsApp ordering, delivery by 12:30. Price ₹150/meal, ₹3,000 for 20-day pass (upfront UPI).

## Options

| Option | Model |
|--------|-------|
| **A** (as pitched) | Start with 3 cooks, 60 meals/day, WhatsApp, ₹150/meal |
| **B** (Chair reframe) | Start with 1 cook, 20 meals/day, payment upfront only, 12:30 hard deadline, prove unit economics before scaling |
| **C** (control) | Don't build it; workers stay with canteens/Swiggy/home food |

## Classified facts

**India tier:** India 2 (assumed) — young office professionals, corporate jobs, UPI-ready

**Idea type:** Local business (primary)

**Today's workaround:** Canteens in offices, Swiggy/Zomato (₹250+), carrying food from home

**Money flow:** ₹150/meal or ₹3,000/20-day pass (upfront via UPI). First 20 customers: post in 2 office WhatsApp groups + free samples at gate for 1 week

**Resources:** ₹60,000, 3 months

**Success (assumed):** 20 customers month 1, break-even month 2, 50+ month 3

**Founder's lean:** YES (5 colleagues stated they'd pay ₹150)

**Evidence:** Stated intent, no payment yet (level 1-2 on evidence ladder)
