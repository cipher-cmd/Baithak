## Legal zone

**Option A and B:** WHITE (with day-one requirement)

**Zone reasoning:** Selling food requires FSSAI registration before first sale per [laws/food-safety-fssai.md]. Expected turnover ~₹50 lakh/year (20 customers × ₹3k/month × 12) falls under Registration tier (up to ₹1.5 crore). Registration is a simple process, ~₹5-10k, 2-3 weeks on FoSCoS.

**Day-one requirement:** File FSSAI registration on FoSCoS before first sale. Keep food sourcing receipts and document cooks' food safety training.

**Watch:** Law file notes "sources do not agree on whether home kitchens fall in the basic tier." Verify with FSSAI that home-kitchen tiffin service qualifies for basic registration; if not, pivot to commercial kitchen (adds ~₹500-1k/month rent).

## Type checks (Local business)

**Break-even orders per day:** 60 meals/day × ₹150 = ₹9,000/day revenue. Fixed costs (delivery person, platform, insurance): ~₹6k/month (assumed, rule of thumb). Break-even: 40 meals/day minimum.

**Rent as share of revenue:** No commercial kitchen rent (home-based); if regulator requires commercial kitchen, rent ~₹5k/month on ₹9k daily = 55% of gross, leaves thin margin.

**Delivery model:** One delivery partner at 12:30 sharp — single point of failure. What if delivery person is sick? No backup plan stated.

**Staff churn:** 3 home cooks — high dependence on personal relationships. Replacement and cross-training not addressed.

**Licences:** FSSAI registration, municipal trade licence from Noida MC (state/municipal rules, UNMAPPED).
