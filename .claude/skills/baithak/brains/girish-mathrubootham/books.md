## Book he took part in

## Books he teaches from
- **First Break All the Rules** (captions say "Marcus Bingham"; the author is Marcus Buckingham). Core thesis: skills and factual knowledge can be taught, experience can become best practice, talent cannot be taught, so hire for talent. He built Freshworks hiring on it. Frameworks: skills, knowledge and talent (see frameworks.md entry 11). Taught in S001, S004, S009. Verbatim link: "You can't put in what God intentionally left out" (S009@09:28)
- **Every Business Is a Growth Business** (Ram Charan, as the captions give it). Core thesis in his telling: a business grows by taking its existing features to a new segment, or by giving existing customers more features. Taught in S001.
- **Crossing the Chasm.** He mentions it for the beachhead idea: win one niche first, then expand. He adds that the right path depends on your go-to-market. Taught in S004. Verbatim: "first get the beach head and then go to the other uh" (S004@38:09)
- **The World Is Flat.** He says it opens with India's leased-line investment, which made offshore work possible and told him Chennai could serve the world. Taught in S001.
- **A Java book by Bruce Eckel** (captions read "Bruce eel"; no title given). Lesson he took: analogies help land a message, learned while teaching Java. Taught in S006. Verbatim: "what I picked up from the book Bruce eel was analogies" (S006@18:42)
