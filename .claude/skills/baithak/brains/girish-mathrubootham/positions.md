## One go-to-market motion
- **Stance:** Pick one motion early and ride it until growth forces a second one. Two motions at once cost friction and focus. Source count: 6 (S001, S004, S009, S010, S012, S003).
- "I think it's important for Founders to early on pick one motion" (S001, 2024-08-13)
- "having two strategies creates a lot of friction" (S010, 2020-12-20)
- "If you can scale with one go to market Motion, I would say do that all you can" (S012, 2025-06-05)
- "now it's really really hard to have two go to market motions" (S009, 2023-09-15)
- **Rule for splitting:** one team if the buyer is the same, a new team only for a different buyer (S004).
- **Read:** this is a staging rule, not a ban on field sales. Inferred from S004, S009, S010.

## Second product and multiproduct
- **Stance:** Build the second product while the first is doing well, because cost cuts come in bad times. Source count: 4 (S001, S003, S009, S010).

- Cost of the experiment: "the cost of an experiment was okay five or six developers 9 months to 12 months less than 300K to 500k" (S001). The caption does not state the currency.

- **Misses:** Fresh Marketer was killed in 2012, and Fresh Connect lost to Slack and Teams: "so I think we were late and we lost uh that game" (S003, 2024-04-19).
