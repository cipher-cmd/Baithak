# Books: Deepinder Goyal

Built from 13 sources (S002 to S010, S012 to S015; S001 and S011 excluded). No source names a commercial business book that he wrote. His written work is an internal culture handbook and a run of shareholder letters, listed below with the one book he recommends.

## Culture (internal handbook)
- **Year:** no recorded position on the exact year. The handbook rests on about a decade of principles (S004).
- **Core thesis:** Write down how people should think, behave and grow, so culture can do the work process would otherwise do. New hires read it several times, and it works as a roadmap for self growth. About 80 percent of the core principles are in it, with more planned for a second version (paraphrase of S004).
- **Quote:** "Culture book was more of a handbook for employees." (S004@09:07)
- **Key frameworks in my words:** culture led, not process led; hire for the organisation, not a role; strict filter at hiring so the wrong people self-select out (see frameworks.md 3 and 7).
- **Taught by:** S004. Related practice in S002 and S003.

## The Art of Thinking Clearly (he recommends it, did not write it)
- **Year:** no recorded position. The author is not named in the source.
- **Core thesis (his reading):** humans are biased and not rational, and a set of about 70 to 100 heuristics can improve decisions. It is the book he gifts most (paraphrase of S004).
- **Quote:** "Humans have a lot of biases. We're not rational." (S004@40:49)
- **Taught by:** S004. His own phrase "First principles thinking." also appears there.

## Written essays and letters (not books)
- **Zomato rebrand letter to shareholders (2025-02-06):** the company was "accidental", culture should drive outcomes more than strategy, and the new name is a mission statement (S012).
- **Step-down letter (2026-01-21):** operating decisions move to Albinder Dhindsa, and the founder keeps strategy, culture, leadership development, and ethics and governance (S014). Unvested ESOPs return to the pool (S014, S015).
- **AI interview (2026-04-28):** the "general versus habitual transactions" test and the "market expansion" test for AI (S013).
