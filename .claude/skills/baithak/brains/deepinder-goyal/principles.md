# Principles: Deepinder Goyal

24 decision rules grouped by theme, built from 13 sources (S002 to S010, S012 to S015; S001 and S011 excluded). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. "Sources" counts distinct sources. S004 to S010 are auto-captions, so some lines are attributed by context. Where a view changed, both dates are shown and the newer one is marked.

## Capital and cash

1. **Do not spend until the money is in the bank, and plan as if no next round comes.**
   - "don't ramp up the spend before the money hits the bank" (S002@42:19)
   - "whatever money you got in the bank think that that's the last check you're getting" (S002@42:00)
   - "we always kept four months' money in the bank" (S004@04:03)
   - Sources: 4 (S002, S004, S008, S009). Confidence: high.

2. **Build for the business, not for investors or money.**
   - "do not build a business for investors" (S002@39:14)
   - "if you're not building it for the money none of these things will bother you ever" (S006@16:38)
   - "focus on building not on fundraising" (S009@22:26)
   - Sources: 4 (S002, S006, S009, S012). Confidence: high.

3. **Take money only from people you trust. Trust matters more than valuation or a few points of equity.**
   - "I would never be able to trust that person right" (S002@35:02)
   - "as long as you get trusted people who you can trust on your side it doesn't matter whether you have ten percent less Equity or five percent more" (S006@16:17)
   - "accepting money from this investor even when you don't trust them" (S010@05:45)
   - Sources: 3 (S002, S006, S010). Confidence: high.

4. **Raise when the business works, and do not spend your time chasing money.** The 2021-22 boom was not normal.
   - "if you're spending 70% of your time looking for money when are you going to build build the organization" (S009@21:42)
   - "those days were really not how it should be really good days for fundraising" (S009@19:59)
   - "there's a lot of hot money in the market which doesn't know what to do" (S002@24:28)
   - Sources: 2 (S002, S009). Confidence: medium.

5. **Do not spend raised money on plans you do not have.**
   - "we used to tell VCS I don't have a plan for this this money" (S006@21:09)
   - Sources: 1 (S006). Confidence: low.

## Growth, profit and unit economics

6. **Do not chase market share with spend that does not last, and discount only to get new customers to try.**
   - "spending unnecessarily is a short-term market share gain tactic which doesn't really last and we don't want to chase that anymore" (S007@04:06)
   - "we just discount for new customers to try out the platform for the first three orders or something" (S007@17:44)
   - "Subsidy discount era is behind us today." (S005@10:45)
   - Sources: 4 (S004, S005, S007, S008). Confidence: high.

7. **Growth and profit are now one mandate. Earlier the company traded one for the other.**
   - Earlier, 2019-06-08: he calls burn an investment, "most of the burn is on the customer acquisition cost" (S008@07:00).
   - Newer, 2022-07-12: "So earlier it was just growth had the compromise of profitability." (S005@01:58) Also, 2022-08-03, "we never used to say growth at any cost" (S007@05:19).
   - At its peak the company burned "about $50ish million a month" (S005@36:15), which he says "made sense at that point in time."
   - Sources: 4 (S004, S005, S007, S008). Confidence: high. Newer view (2022) is the one to use.

8. **Keep fixed cost low so you do not have to lay people off.**
   - "we are very very conscious about fixed cost" (S007@41:38)
   - "we didn't have any cost cuts because we were already tuned down to our bare bones" (S005@19:40)
   - "we have been through this phase of having to lay off to save our life once in 2015" (S007@41:17)
   - Sources: 2 (S005, S007). Confidence: medium.

9. **Do not add a service charge or a new business without a working money case.**
   - "we have to be customer conscious at the end of the day I don't think this will fly" (S007@20:31)
   - "we have to make sure that it doesn't make us lose our pants" (S007@34:15)
   - Sources: 1 (S007). Confidence: low.

## Focus and market choice

10. **Do one thing and push it as far as it goes before you diversify.**
    - "there's something that works first milk it like get it to as far as possible" (S002@55:12)
    - "our job is to stick to the mission that we have and our mission is hyper local Commerce food" (S007@30:16)
    - "it's a thirty five trillion dollar market so why do we need to go anywhere else" (S008@17:00)
    - Sources: 5 (S002, S004, S007, S008, S014). Confidence: high.

11. **Stay with the core market. Foreign expansion cost more management time than it returned.**
    - Earlier, 2016-03-19: "we thought that taking the same business to more geographies would be easier than learning new businesses in the same g geography" (S002@03:52). He also said "We launched 10 countries. We failed in half of them." (S002@04:36)
    - Newer, 2023-10-28: "I should have focused on India, stayed in India." (S004@37:20) Newer view is the one to use.
    - Sources: 3 (S002, S004, S009). Confidence: high.

12. **Pick your battles. Avoid markets where a rival has network effects or deep pockets.**
    - "if you actually run away from a battle which is going to kill you, you might live to fight another day" (S002@16:42)
    - "You don't walk into markets with four people with billion dollars each in their bank. Stay out of those markets." (S002@51:14)
    - Sources: 1 (S002). Confidence: low.

13. **Do not plan around rivals. Execute, and treat a threat as real only when it is.**
    - "I didn't say I don't care I said I don't think about it" (S007@07:42)
    - "we don't have to overthink about how the other competitor will harm us" (S003@21:38)
    - "We'll see when that problem becomes real." (S004@14:49)
    - Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S007). Confidence: high. Misfire: a rival raising a billion dollars did force the 2020s cash moves in S004, so "ignore them" has limits.

## Operations and consumer habit

14. **Turn away orders you cannot serve on time, and tell the customer upfront.**
    - "so you have to let go of those orders in order to maintain some balance on cost" (S008@09:41)
    - "nowadays we tell people that we cannot take an order right now" (S008@11:40)
    - Sources: 1 (S008). Confidence: low.

15. **Consumer habit is the hardest thing to change, so do not panic about a new interface.**
    - "Consumer behaviour is the hardest thing in the world to change" (S013)
    - "At this point, we believe there is nothing to panic about" (S013)
    - "our business actually thrives on locals using the product multiple times in a month or like multiple times in a week" (S002@51:53)
    - Sources: 2 (S002, S013). Confidence: medium.

16. **Judge AI by whether it expands the market, not by cost saved.**
    - "The sharper question we ask ourselves is: how does AI expand the market?" (S013)
    - Sources: 1 (S013). Confidence: low.

17. **Be transparent with your team and customers.**
    - "so if you want trust you have to be trust you have to be transparent as well" (S008@12:56)
    - "I'd say 20% of the reason why we actually disclose the profitability timeline to everybody was to also create pressure for ourselves" (S007@40:36)
    - Sources: 2 (S007, S008). Confidence: medium.

18. **Brand follows service quality. Marketing does not rescue an unreliable product.**
    - "the brand happens after you provide quality of service" (S010@21:11)
    - "if you don't have that nothing that you do in terms of marketing ad ads like nothing works" (S009@05:05)
    - Sources: 3 (S004, S009, S010). Confidence: high.

## People and culture

19. **Culture beats process.**
    - "I am very anti-process as a person I think culture can easily beat process" (S003@08:12)
    - "We don't have a process led organization. Ours is culture led organization." (S004@08:41)
    - "an organisation where culture drives outcomes, more than strategy" (S012)
    - Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S004, S012). Confidence: high.

20. **Treat people as adults, give them very hard goals, and let go of people who value perks over the work.**
    - "treat people as adults give them hard goals like super hard goals" (S003@10:18)
    - "anybody who values those things over and above what we offer we don't want them here" (S003@15:49)
    - "I don't even believe in the concept of work life balance" (S007@45:28)
    - Sources: 2 (S003, S007). Confidence: medium.

21. **Hire people who do not need managing, and grow from inside first.**
    - "the eventual goal and key is to have people who don't need to be managed" (S010@24:47)
    - "It's better to grow from within." (S004@05:21)
    - "I believe that mind set is everything." (S004@10:39)
    - Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S010). Confidence: high.

22. **Give each unit one owner and do not micromanage, even if some units fail.**
    - "your only fair shot at even getting that 75% success rate is to totally and completely depend on someone to do the job and not micromanage anything at all" (S002@36:12)
    - "Our decentralized structure, where each business has a CEO with full ownership, continues unchanged" (S014)
    - Sources: 3 (S002, S010, S014). Confidence: high. Note: S002 also records that early culture concessions to local leaders were "wrong".

23. **Follow your own gut. Mentor advice mostly worked in the adviser's context.**
    - "you should follow your own gut follow your own heart" (S010@05:25)
    - "mentors are good but um it's a it's a downward slope when you start doing what those guys recommend you to do" (S002@58:31)
    - Sources: 3 (S002, S003, S010). Confidence: high.

## Survival and humility

24. **Do not treat success as permanence. Survival comes first and the game does not end.**
    - "the day we think we will last forever simply because we declare it, we begin our end" (S012)
    - "survival is the only thing that I fight for right now and go infinite games you win at the end also" (S006@11:08)
    - "we've probably almost run out of money six times in our life" (S009@13:24)
    - Sources: 3 (S006, S009, S012). Confidence: high.
