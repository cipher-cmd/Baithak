# Positions: Deepinder Goyal

Built from the extract notes in build/deepinder-goyal/notes/ (batch 01 and batch 02). Sources used: S002 to S010, S012 to S015. S001 and S011 are excluded. Each stance has a verbatim quote, a source id and a date (the upload or article date from `sources.md`). Where his view changed, both versions sit side by side and the newer one is marked. Topics with no evidence say so. Lines marked "attribution by context" come from auto-captions with no speaker labels and are not quoted.

## Consumer habit and daily use

- **Stance:** The business lives on locals who order again and again. Frequency is the test of a daily habit, not a tourist product.
- "our business actually thrives on locals using the product multiple times in a month or like multiple times in a week" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "Consumer behaviour is the hardest thing in the world to change" (S013, 2026-04-28)
- **Read:** frequent use is the moat. Inferred from S002, S013.
- **Source count:** 2 (S002, S013).

## Chat and AI

- **Stance:** AI is a tool for the company. Chat is a poor front door for frequent, complex transactions, and he does not expect chatbots to take the habit away.
- "AI is our friend, not a threat" (S013, 2026-04-28)
- "They are poor interfaces for complex, high-frequency, habitual transactions" (S013, 2026-04-28)
- "The sharper question we ask ourselves is: how does AI expand the market?" (S013, 2026-04-28)
- "At this point, we believe there is nothing to panic about" (S013, 2026-04-28). This is a company statement and a hedge.
- **Competition in transactions:** Google spent over a decade on transactions and did not dislodge Booking.com, Expedia or Amazon (S013, 2026-04-28).
- **Source count:** 1 (S013), written piece.

## Market size and market choice

- **Stance:** Market size is restaurants multiplied by currency strength, not population. Stay out of a market where a large local player has network effects, or buy that player (inferred from S002).
- "For us the number of restaurants into the currency strength is actually the market size." (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "it's very hard for us to win that space because of the network effect of that local business out there" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- **Source count:** 2 (S002, S008).

## Hyperlocal and niche

- **Stance:** The business is hyperlocal and runs on on-the-ground sales and marketing in each city. Each location is tested before it is scaled.
- "it's a hyper local business for us, right?" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "once we have it really figured out that's when we are going to go and scale it" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- "we actually understand that this kitchen whatever is the formula that works here might not be the formula that works on the road next to us" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- **Source count:** 3 (S002, S007, S008).

## Expansion beyond India (view changed)

- **Earlier view (2016 to 2019):** take the business abroad, and treat India as a market with a ceiling. "we thought that taking the same business to more geographies would be easier than learning new businesses in the same g geography" (S002, 2016-03-19). In 2019: "we said that 20 cities in India is where the market lies right and that's it it's not going to go beyond 20" (S008, 2019-06-08).
- **Newer view (2023):** India first. "I should have focused on India, stayed in India." (S004, 2023-10-28). He also says "I think India will become next economic super power in the next 10-20 years." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- **Newer view, on management time:** "Why should I spend 50% of my time for 10% business? Doesn't work." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- **Newer view (2024):** "it's a great time right now to be in India building businesses in India" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- **Source count:** 4 (S002, S004, S008, S009). Newer view marked above.

## Capital and raising money

- **Stance:** Raise money from a business that works, not for a better market later. Treat the cash in the bank as the last money you will get, and never ramp spend before money lands.
- "you have to build a business and raise money now" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- "whatever money you got in the bank think that that's the last check you're getting" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "don't ramp up the spend before the money hits the bank" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "investments are more or less done on basis of the cash you had on the bank" (S008, 2019-06-08). This describes how investors decide, not his own rule.
- **Source count:** 4 (S002, S006, S008, S009).

## Runway and cash reserve

- **Stance:** Keep a fixed cash cushion. He says the company does not worry about runway because it has been close to running out before.
- "we always kept four months' money in the bank" (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "we're a company which doesn't worry about runway at all because we've been very very close to running out of money" (S008, 2019-06-08)
- "we've probably almost run out of money six times in our life" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- **Source count:** 3 (S004, S008, S009).

## Fundraising time and bootstrapping

- **Stance:** Context decides. Some businesses are better built bootstrapped. He says a founder who spends his week chasing money is not building the company.
- "there's some businesses which are better off being built in a bootstrap fashion" (S003, 2023-06-15)
- "if you're spending 70% of your time looking for money when are you going to build build the organization" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- "focus on building not on fundraising" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- "we bootstrapped for 2 years and then we got our first round of funding" (S010, 2024-08-09)
- **Read:** inferred from S009 and S010, the company bootstrapped for about two and a half years before outside money.
- **Source count:** 4 (S003, S009, S010, S002).

## Investors and trust

- **Stance:** Trust matters more than valuation. He would give up a small valuation gap to avoid an investor he does not trust.
- "I would never be able to trust that person right" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "as long as you get trusted people who you can trust on your side it doesn't matter whether you have ten percent less Equity or five percent more" (S006, 2023-06-12)
- "we used to tell VCS I don't have a plan for this this money" (S006, 2023-06-12). He says do not spend raised money on plans you do not have.
- **Source count:** 3 (S002, S006, S010).

## Partners and suppliers

- **Stance:** The company needs far more restaurant partners than it can build itself, so partners are part of the plan.
- "we need about a million more restaurants and we cannot build it by ourselves" (S008, 2019-06-08)

## Pricing and discounting

- **Stance:** Make food cheaper, healthier and faster. Restaurants set their own prices, and discounts should be visible and short.
- "it's about making the food cheaper healthier faster" (S008, 2019-06-08)
- "we just discount for new customers to try out the platform for the first three orders or something" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- "It should be Rs. 80 off." (S004, 2023-10-28). He wants discounts as fixed rupee amounts.
- "I want to change it, but I don't call this discounting honest." (S004, 2023-10-28). He means percentage discounts that look bigger than they are.
- **Numbers:** discounts were cut by 40 percent (S008, 2019-06-08). Net discount was about 12 percent in 2022 (S007, 2022-08-03).
- **Source count:** 3 (S004, S007, S008).

## Unit economics

- **Stance:** Profit per order is small and comes from scale. Fixed costs stay flat while orders grow, so cost per order falls.
- "So our blended commission comes upto Rs. 80." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "If my scale becomes 10X, then my cost per order reduces." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "we used to be cash flow positive by the way always" (S006, 2023-06-12)
- **Spending to chase share:** "spending unnecessarily is a short-term market share gain tactic which doesn't really last and we don't want to chase that anymore" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- **Source count:** 4 (S004, S006, S007, S008).

- "we think that's a better business in terms of customer cohorts in terms of um potential scale" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- **Blinkit versus Zomato:** "I don't have ego in terms of which business should be big bigger as long as both are growing as much as they can" (S007, 2022-08-03)

## Brand

- **Stance:** A brand is built by service quality. Marketing only works on top of consistent service.
- "I think a brand is all about consistency and quality of service" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- "the brand happens after you provide quality of service" (S010, 2024-08-09)
- "if you don't have that nothing that you do in terms of marketing ad ads like nothing works" (S009, 2024-01-14)
- **Early and later views agree:** the brand came from accidents, not a plan. "zomato's brand is cool but it's all over the place" (S006, 2023-06-12). "Zomato is an accidental company. It was born out of a simple desire to serve, and over time, it became a business." (S012, 2025-02-06)
- **Brand by use case:** "customers relate to a brand for a use case that they have in their mind" (S010, 2024-08-09)
- **Source count:** 5 (S006, S009, S010, S012, S003).

- **Negative press:** "is just the cost of the brand that we are building" (S003, 2023-06-15). "you can't protect it you can't save it the only way to save it is to kill the brand" (S003, 2023-06-15). You cannot hide from criticism.
- **Name as mission:** "This isn't just a name change; it is a mission statement." (S012, 2025-02-06). "Eternal is a powerful name, and to be honest, it scares me to my core." (S012, 2025-02-06)

## Paid ads

- **Stance:** Sell ads only where the place is good enough to deliver. He sells banner ads only to places rated above 3.5 out of 5.
- "our sales teams uh make sure that we don't sell ads to places which are less than 3.5 out of five" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "it's a very very 1990s kind of a revenue model which has scaled very well for us" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- **Source count:** 1 (S002).

## Reviews and fraud

- **Stance:** Hide fake reviews, do not delete them. Never delete a bad review for a paying client; let that client go.
- "most of the reviews we actually hide them. We don't delete them." (S002, 2016-03-19)
- "under no circumstances does it make any long-term business sense for us to delete a review for a client which is paying us" (S002, 2016-03-19)
- **Source count:** 1 (S002).

## First hire and internal promotion

- **Stance:** Hire from inside first. Growing an insider takes about three months. An outside head of marketing takes about a year before performance is known.
- "It's better to grow from within." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "It's easier for me to pick someone from inside, spend 3 months to grow him and put him in that role." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "we say we hire for the organization we don't hire for a role" (S003, 2023-06-15)
- **Source count:** 3 (S003, S004, S010).

## Hiring and people quality

- **Stance:** Hire people who do not need managing, and people who are not looking for perks.
- "The kind of people we need, they don't search for jobs." (S004, 2023-10-28)
- "the eventual goal and key is to have people who don't need to be managed" (S010, 2024-08-09)
- "anybody who values those things over and above what we offer we don't want them here" (S003, 2023-06-15)
- **Pay:** "great work should be the Baseline here will like compensate you well" (S003, 2023-06-15). Pay is a baseline, not a lever, and "we have zero bonuses for anyone" (S003, 2023-06-15).
- **Women and returners:** "we haven't cracked it yet" (S003, 2023-06-15). He says the company has not solved returning mothers' ramp-up. On parental leave in 2019: "we should we should be the first ones to change this" (S008, 2019-06-08).

## Firing and layoffs

- **Stance:** Do not lay off. The company laid off once to survive and does not want to repeat it.
- "we don't want to be that company again" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- "we have been through this phase of having to lay off to save our life once in 2015" (S007, 2022-08-03)

- "I am very anti-process as a person I think culture can easily beat process" (S003, 2023-06-15)

## Work hours and work-life balance

- **Stance:** Nobody works around the clock. The founder is first in and last out. He does not believe in work-life balance as a concept.
- "24x7 is not even possible like it's physically not possible you have to sleep" (S003, 2023-06-15)
- "first person in the office always last person to leave for the rest of my life" (S003, 2023-06-15)
- "I don't even believe in the concept of work life balance" (S007, 2022-08-03)
- "People walk in when they want." (S002, 2016-03-19). Meetings still start on time.
- **Changed view on chilling:** "I did chill a little bit um and then I figured out that it's going to be the end of the organization if I start chilling" (S003, 2023-06-15). He went back to long hours.
