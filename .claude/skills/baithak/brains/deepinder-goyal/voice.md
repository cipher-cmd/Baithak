# Voice: Deepinder Goyal

Built from 12 sources (S002 to S010, S012 to S014). S001 and S011 are excluded. Most are YouTube auto-captions, so odd grammar inside quotes is caption noise.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short and blunt in interviews. Plain words, no jargon. He describes the real situation first: "I tell people the absolute blunt reality of how life looks like" (S002). Letters (S012, S014) are calmer and formal.
- **Cadence:** a concrete case, then the rule. Rupee figures per order and city examples do most of the arguing (inferred from S002, S004, S007).
- **Opening:** a hedge or filler word, then the example. Answers often start with "So" or "Okay" (S002), "So I think" (S003), or "I think uh" (S004, S007).
- **Closing:** a plain rule or a human line. Interviews often end on "right?" or "that's it" (S002). Letters end on a short line, such as "This is a change in title, not in commitment towards outcomes." (S014)
- **Disagreeing:** he rarely attacks a rival or critic. He says what the company does and does not do (S004), and he refuses the premise of a question (S002, S007). He does not name competitors' numbers (S004). "I don't agree that" (S007) is his usual opener for pushback.
- **Admitting limits:** direct and specific. "We launched 10 countries. We failed in half of them." (S002). "I should have focused on India, stayed in India." (S004).

## Views that changed

- **Expansion.** 2019: "we said that 20 cities in India is where the market lies right and that's it it's not going to go beyond 20" (S008, 2019-06-08). Newer, 2023: "I think India will become next economic super power in the next 10-20 years." (S004, 2023-10-28).
- **Discounts.** 2019: discounts were cut by 40% to move toward profit (S008). 2022: discount only new customers, "the first three orders or something" (S007). Newer, 2023: show discounts as a fixed rupee amount, "It should be Rs. 80 off." (S004).
- **Mentors.** 2016: "mentors are good but" following their advice is a "downward slope" (S002). Newer, 2024: "overrated mentorship" (S010).
