# Positions: Kunal Shah

24 topics. Built from S001 to S010 notes. Each stance has verbatim quotes with source id and date (upload dates from `sources.md`). Where his view changed, both versions sit side by side with the newer one marked. Topics with no evidence say "no recorded position". Weighted toward the seat question: is this a big enough leap over today's jugaad that nobody goes back.

## Delta 4: the core test
- **Stance:** Score the old way and the new way out of 10 on efficiency. A gap of 4 or more means three things: the switch is irreversible, users tolerate flaws, and users brag about it (the UBP). Below 4 the switch is reversible. Source count: 5 (S001, S002, S005, S007, S010). Most detail in S010 (2016) and S007 (2026).
- "every time you see that the product efficiency delta is greater than or equal to four, three things happen. It is irreversible." (S001@06:52, 2024-03-24)
- "every time the Delta of efficiency is greater than four you unlock part of gold therefore we'll call this the Delta 4 Theory" (S010@06:02, 2016-10-02)
- "when the delta is less than four, what you will discover is that it is reversible. You will not see people bragging about it and you have zero tolerance for it." (S001@08:56, 2024-03-24)
- The score is a rough self-check, not a measurement. He calls it a work in progress in S010.
- **Limits he states:** "this is the last latest version of this that you've seen so far it's work in progress" (S010@34:34, 2016-10-02).

## Delta 4 and tech or AI
- **Stance:** Putting a product on technology does not make it better. Only a real lift in efficiency of the wanted behaviour counts. LLMs and platform shifts are Delta 4. Source count: 5 (S001, S002, S003, S007, S010).
- "just by adding tech it doesn't become delta four right it has to improve the efficiency score of the desired behavior" (S002@54:47, 2022-06-28)
- "it's Delta 4 because the efficiency of doing things before and after is now showing that." (S001@07:49, 2024-03-24)
- "Just build something that people care about and make it significantly better, cheaper, and faster. That's what will create Delta 4" (S007@59:36, 2026-01-08)
- "if they can do a 99% more accuracy or close to human accuracy on doing that blood test then they have made a Delta 4 product" (S010@31:36, 2016-10-02)
- Newer view (S007, 2026): he names AI, smartphones and GPS as the platform shifts that open Delta 4 chances. Older view (S010, 2016): an AI blood test as the example. Same logic, wider scope.

## Jugaad and incumbent workarounds
- **Stance:** An existing workaround such as a maid, a local shop or a family habit is the real competitor. If the app is not at least 4 points better than the workaround, do not build it. Source count: 4 (S002, S003, S007, S010).
- "the more efficient way of getting your laundry is is getting a maid that is Delta 4 not going to a laundry app" (S010@11:36, 2016-10-02)
- "in india we had maids coming at home why do we need a laundry startup" (S002@50:37, 2022-06-28)
- "the reason I don't think we have found a wedding unicorn in India so far because people are not seeking efficiency sometimes, right?" (S003@52:16, 2025-07-26)
- Around 800 laundry startups copied the West, where maids are costly (S010, 2016-10-02). He reads this as copying a model with no gain for Indians.

## What stops a Delta 4 product from winning
- **Stance:** Six limits (S010): affordability, readiness (smartphones), learning effort, micro-configurations (the maid), collective participation (WhatsApp), and branded lanes where the action becomes a verb. Also price: a cheaper option can beat a better one. Source count: 2 (S010, S007).
- "if I create an Uber like app and nobody has got smartphones in the this country will it work no it will not" (S010@13:41, 2016-10-02)
- "Then it may not be Delta 4 because then Rapido's cheaper to go from one point to another point." (S007@60:57, 2026-01-08)
- "whenever the the state change becomes a verb it's very hard to beat this" (S010@15:19, 2016-10-02)
- Timing example: specs are Delta 4 only until LASIK gets cheap (S010, 2016-10-02).

## Irreversibility and the ratchet
- **Stance:** Once people have lived the better experience they do not go back. Source count: 3 (S001, S007, S010).
- "once you experience something high quality it's very hard to reverse." (S007@45:40, 2026-01-08)
- "And it's very hard to go back to a lower star hotels once you've experienced five-star." (S007@45:22, 2026-01-08)
- Delta 4 shifts spread across modes: "as flights became cheaper it did impact trains, it did impact buses, it did impact road travel" (S007@61:14, 2026-01-08)

## Faking Delta 4 (discounts, cashback, branding)
- **Stance:** Discounts and cashback inflate the gap for a while and then it fades. Use discounts only inside a Delta 4 category to take share. Brands can fake the score with influencers, piggybacking, or invented inefficiencies. Source count: 1 (S010).
- "they'll do cash backs and discounts and artificially increase the new behaviors Delta and that does not last right" (S010@10:01, 2016-10-02)
- "share in Delta 1 category it just erods value" (S010@13:15, 2016-10-02)
- "you can fake Delta 4 by the power of marketing and branding" (S010@17:10, 2016-10-02)
- "fmcg companies are brilliant at creating faking inefficiencies" (S010@19:42, 2016-10-02)

## Delta 1 to 3 (ordinary improvements)
- **Stance:** A Delta 1 product has a small gap. He says a funded Delta 1 company erodes value, and that mediocre founders on Delta 4 beat great founders on Delta 1. No definition of Delta 2 or Delta 3 is recorded. Source count: 1 (S010).
- "things I have seen no mediocre Founders do extraordinarily well with Delta 4 spaces versus great Founders working on Delta 1 ideas and losing" (S010@26:43, 2016-10-02)
- "any place which has large sales teams is is selling a Delta 1 product through the Delta 4 efficiency of distribution" (S010@33:50, 2016-10-02)
- Delta 2 and Delta 3 meaning: no recorded position.

## Real motivation first: rivers and dams
- **Stance:** Efficiency without a real human motivation destroys wealth. First find where demand already flows (the river), then build the product (the dam). Source count: 4 (S002, S006, S007, S008).
- "Most tech founders are great at building dams and terrible at finding rivers." (S007@21:55, 2026-01-08)
- "you can make something efficient that nobody cares about" (S008@41:22, 2025-09-17)
- "Rivers don't come. Rivers exist and understanding of rivers comes from the human motivation." (S007@00:53, 2026-01-08)
- "tech founders are in the business of making it extremely easy to deliver an existing consumer motivation" (S007@20:01, 2026-01-08)

## Testing demand
- **Stance:** Ask who will pay and how much, down to one rupee. Test with family. Test a prototype before coding. Do not ask friends for a score because they sugar-coat. Source count: 4 (S002, S004, S007, S010).
- "Will they pay 1 rupee?" (S007@62:30, 2026-01-08)
- "ask them would you pay money for it" (S004@64:54, 2020-09-18)
- "only when 95% of people said yes we started building the product" (S010@23:36, 2016-10-02)
- "in India we are factories of sugar coating" (S010@22:38, 2016-10-02)
- "your mom pay for this will your cfo pay for this will your kid pay for this" (S002@65:53, 2022-06-28)

## Time-saving products in India
- **Stance:** Do not sell convenience or time saving to Indian consumers. Indians do not price an hour, so these products rarely monetise. Status and emotion earn the margin. Source count: 6 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S007, S008).
- "products that sell convenience and save you your time rarely monetize" (S002@17:49, 2022-06-28)
- "nobody in in India has been paid an hourly salary" (S003@70:40, 2025-07-26)
- "it's very hard to build SaaS for India because you don't value time." (S007@26:21, 2026-01-08)
- "there is no country that has become prosperous without becoming obsessed with value of time" (S008@18:40, 2025-09-17)
- Nuance: a boring transaction people hate (bills, fuel, recharge) is the exception, since they want less time and money spent there (S006, S008).

## India 1 versus Bharat (broad market against top spenders)
- **Stance:** He does not use the labels India 1, India 2, India 3 or Bharat in these sources. His framing is the top 25 to 40 million people who value time and have money. Build for them, not for a lowest common denominator that does not pay. Source count: 5 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S008). Inferred from S001, S002, S008.
- "the value of time and per capita income is concentrated with 25 million families and therefore focusing on them is important." (S001@33:47, 2024-03-24)
- "And at least the top 30 40 million customers care about this a lot." (S008@22:30, 2025-09-17)
- "India becomes a great MAU farm but very low on ARPU." (S001@24:28, 2024-03-24)
- "if you remove the top 30 million families or 30 million individuals the per capita income would drop to maybe 600" (S002@35:33, 2022-06-28)
- Newer view (S008, 2025-09-17): solve one slice and the next is ready. "If you make it work for 100 200 million, if their life changes, the next 200 million is ready."
- Older view (S009, 2021-07-28): "it cannot be by serving indian customers just the market does not have depth". The notes flag this as a dated 2021 stance.
- India 1, 2 and 3 as named groups: no recorded position.

## Free, freebies and willingness to pay
- **Stance:** Mass Indian users take freebies and do not pay. Pricing needs a free or ad-funded layer, or a cross-sell. Payment rails that charged fees did not take off. Source count: 3 (S001, S002, S007).
- "india is a country where you give them freebies and and you get all your dius you want you do not get your rpo" (S002@37:26, 2022-06-28)
- "when we had charged one percent two percent mdr for do that it never took off" (S002@41:11, 2022-06-28)
- "imagine I announce 100 rupees for short videos. Nobody will pay for it because people fundamentally believe it's a wrong use of their time." (S007@62:45, 2026-01-08)

## Status, brand and where margin lives
- **Stance:** Gross margin exists where a product lets people signal or jump status. Pure utility drifts to zero margin. Source count: 4 (S002, S004, S007, S003). Inferred from S002, S004, S007.
- "gross margins exist when you allow human beings to jump their social status and gross margins disappear when you do not help them increase their social status" (S002@24:07, 2022-06-28)
- "turns out the gross margin was 3x more the reason is in a society we care more about showing to others" (S002@69:14, 2022-06-28)
- "products that increase your status will always be higher gross margin and you can either increase the status or prevent status loss" (S004@62:15, 2020-09-18)
- **Tension in his own view:** he says status-obsessed people are unlikely to create wealth (S007, 2026) while saying status products carry the margin (S002, 2022). Inferred from S002, S007: use status as the buyer's motive, do not be consumed by it yourself.

## Copying Western or Chinese models
- **Stance:** Do not copy another country's model or profit pool. Check what Indians actually pay for. Source count: 4 (S001, S002, S004, S010).
- "trying to copy somebody else's profit pool to your country will not be a wise idea" (S001@36:34, 2024-03-24)
- "nothing between india and china is common except our population that's where the similarity ends" (S002@56:26, 2022-06-28)
- "we copy the concepts from the West where you cannot hire a maid" (S010@11:36, 2016-10-02)

## Focus versus a super app
- **Stance:** Two sides. Start with one cohort and one small product (a knife, not a Swiss knife). Once trust exists, cross-sell widely, because low-trust markets and low revenue per user push toward many products. Source count: 4 (S001, S002, S008, S009). Inferred from S002, S009.
- "becoming everything for everyone is the fastest way to die" (S009@43:15, 2021-07-28)
- "focus is almost a curse in asian markets" (S002@34:10, 2022-06-28)
- "you make very little per user, so you have to do many things." (S001@28:24, 2024-03-24)
- Cohort rule: "we have to change the country by cohort by cohort by cohort." (S008@15:50, 2025-09-17)

## Trust
- **Stance:** In low-trust markets trust concentrates in a few names, which can launch unrelated products. Serve the trustworthy group first. Source count: 3 (S001, S002, S004).
- "there is concentration of trust." (S001@29:04, 2024-03-24)
- "tata group is this concentration of trust and they can launch a car to a refrigerator to salt to furniture" (S002@29:31, 2022-06-28)
- "we said that we will focus on more trustworthy individuals to increase the trust of the system" (S002@57:13, 2022-06-28)

## Changing consumer behaviour
- **Stance:** To change behaviour add an incentive and reward good behaviour, do not punish. Removing friction alone does not change motivation. Source count: 3 (S006, S007, S003).
- "whenever we are trying to change behavior we have to always put incentive to change the behavior" (S006@18:35, 2021-09-10)
- "friction of investing going away will not change investing behavior." (S007@57:21, 2026-01-08)
- "just making things accessible does not change the core motivation." (S007@58:57, 2026-01-08)
- India reacts to the unfamiliar with fear: "as a country to things that we don't understand is usually fear or mock" (S003@58:26, 2025-07-26)

## Boring versus interesting transactions
- **Stance:** People want to cut time and money on boring transactions (bills, fuel, recharge) and spend on interesting ones. Build to remove the pain in the boring ones. Source count: 2 (S006, S008).
- "trying to reduce time and money spent on all boring things and invest in all the transactions that are interesting" (S006@16:07, 2021-09-10)
- "because I can assure you there's not a single human being who's enjoyed making a payment." (S008@56:58, 2025-09-17)
- "My uh wish for you is that the best payment should be not seen." (S008@56:58, 2025-09-17)

## Personal finance and health apps
- **Stance:** A bad business. Founders love the topic and wrongly think users do. Retention is poor because looking at your own money causes anxiety. Source count: 1 (S007).
- "most people who do personal finance apps uh they love doing personal finance themselves, so they think everybody likes doing that." (S007@63:01, 2026-01-08)
- "It's the stupidest business to build in my view." (S007@63:24, 2026-01-08)

## Pricing and discounting
- **Stance:** Price high where the buyer wants to differentiate, and discount only in a Delta 4 category. He dislikes MRP as socialistic. Source count: 3 (S003, S004, S010).
- "it multiple x more. uh than the regular Mahindi so that people feel that okay not only is it differentiated it's expensive so it must be great" (S003@06:07, 2025-07-26)
- "MRP removes this and it's a very socialistic approach" (S004@13:12, 2020-09-18)
- "share in Delta 1 category it just erods value" (S010@13:15, 2016-10-02)

## Brand, ads and marketing
- **Stance:** Do not spoon-feed. Smart audiences want mystery. Aim at the household's tech decision maker, the 8 to 20 year old. No big celebrities. Great ads spread before TV. Source count: 2 (S007, S008).
- "all ads are unfortunately spoon-feeding." (S007@48:24, 2026-01-08)
- "Smart people want to be left with a little bit of mystery and they want to discover" (S008@08:41, 2025-09-17)
- "I call them the CTOs of the households." (S008@07:57, 2025-09-17)
- "AB experimentation is a sign of deep insecurity." (S007@29:33, 2026-01-08)

## Raising money and TAM
- **Stance:** Capital buys speed at the price of equity. Raise only if the market is big enough for a very large outcome. Indian TAM thinking is weak. Source count: 1 (S003).
- "So unless you have a big TAM, you should not seek investment" (S003@79:37, 2025-07-26)
- "but capital is not the reason to start the business right" (S003@77:03, 2025-07-26)
- "our understanding of TAM is very weak." (S003@80:27, 2025-07-26)

## Shutting things down
- **Stance:** If you cannot lift a Delta 1 product to Delta 4, shut it down. Killing or selling a company is normal. Source count: 1 (S010).
- "in this country we have not been trained to shut things down" (S010@32:31, 2016-10-02)
- "even if I can make uh 5,000 companies shut down I'll be very happy" (S010@34:56, 2016-10-02)

## Hiring
- **Stance:** Hire for slope, compounding, ambiguity tolerance and teaching. Hire opposites, not clones. Ambition cannot be installed. Source count: 4 (S002, S003, S007, S001).
- "I think you have to hire for your weaknesses." (S007@35:57, 2026-01-08)
- "It's very hard to make somebody ambitious also." (S003@37:03, 2025-07-26)
- "Because all the people who hate ambiguity become insane liabilities in the early stage." (S007@34:57, 2026-01-08)
- "if they cannot use that, leverage that, they'll become a liability in 5 years anyways." (S001@32:11, 2024-03-24)

## Competition and crowded markets
- **Stance:** He avoids crowded markets and calls competing zero-sum. Pick a market with tailwinds. Source count: 3 (S009, S002, S004).
- "the true joy in life is to not really compete" (S009@43:58, 2021-07-28)
- "if you meet the bad market you will die but if you find a market that has used tailwinds to it even a mediocre founder will do really really well" (S002@44:41, 2022-06-28)
- Global firms often arrive after Indian startups and grow bigger in the same space (S004, 2020-09-18).

## Remote work
- **Stance:** Creative work and bonding need in-person time. This is a 2021 view, and no newer position is recorded. Source count: 2 (S009, S005).
- "it's very hard to do creative stuff and innovative stuff remotely" (S009@21:30, 2021-07-28)
- "humans need other humans presence in person" (S009@14:23, 2021-07-28)
- In S005 (2021-07-20) he said young people should return to the office to learn, and he was not confident that work from home would stay permanent.

## Wealth: zero-sum or not (his view changed)
- **Older view (2021):** "if you make money multiple people can exist" (S006@22:50, 2021-09-10)
- **Newer view (2022, S002) and repeated in 2024 (S001):** "we often say that wealth is not zero sum but it actually is because wealth is nothing but energy stored" (S002@115:53, 2022-06-28). "wealth is nothing but storage of energy." (S001@63:12, 2024-03-24)
- Note: S001 (2024) also says "To me, wealth is nothing but information asymmetry." (S001@51:24, 2024-03-24). Both newer lines move away from a plain "not zero-sum" framing.
