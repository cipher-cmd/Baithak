## What it says
The Ministry of Education's 2024 coaching guidelines are a model for States and UTs to adopt through their own legal framework (PIB, February 2024). They cover registration, tutor qualifications, fees, a prospectus, a complaint mechanism and penalties. A UGC notice dated 31 August 2026 invites applications for recognition to offer open, distance or online programmes from 2026-27. The CCPA's 2022 misleading-ad guidelines (9 June 2022, under section 18 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019) cover claims and endorsements.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: you award or sell a degree from an institution without UGC recognition for online or distance mode. Make no claim of being a university you are not. Misleading claims about results, ranks or jobs also fall here.
- GRAY if: you run a coaching centre and your State has not adopted the 2024 guidelines.
- Compliant route: partner with an institution holding UGC recognition for online or distance mode, and show its recognition. Publish refund terms. Back every result, rank or job claim with evidence.

## Watch for
- The 2024 guideline PDF on education.gov.in returned 404 and was not opened. The PIB release confirms adoption, complaints and penalties, but not age, refund or fine terms. Reports disagree: an under-16 bar, or completion of secondary school. A 45-day refund figure is from a Karnataka draft.
- CCPA 2022 guidelines: official PDF not opened. Fine amounts conflict across secondary sources (Rs 10 lakh, rising to Rs 50 lakh for repeats, per one; Rs 10 to 50 lakh per another).
- The UGC notice opened, but its PDF gave no extractable text. The "entitled" versus "recognised" distinction and the franchise ban are not verified.
