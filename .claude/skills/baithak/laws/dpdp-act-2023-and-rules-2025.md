## What it says
The DPDP Act 2023 governs how businesses ("data fiduciaries") collect and use personal data of people in India. The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 with a phased rollout of about 18 months:
- The Data Protection Board provisions took effect at once.
- Consent manager registration starts around November 2026.
- Most duties apply from about May 2027: notice and consent, breach reporting, and verifiable parental consent for children's data.

## What it means for a founder
- GRAY if: the product collects personal data. Most duties are not enforceable yet, but they will be soon. Build for them now: a clear notice, a recorded consent, deletion on request, breach response, and no tracking or targeted ads aimed at children.
- BLACK (once the duties apply): processing children's data without verifiable parental consent, behavioural tracking or targeted advertising directed at children, or using data beyond what the user consented to.
- Compliant route: consent logs from day one, data minimisation, and a named grievance contact.

## Watch for
The exact phase dates in the Gazette (sources differ by a day), Data Protection Board guidance, and any change to which businesses are "significant data fiduciaries".
