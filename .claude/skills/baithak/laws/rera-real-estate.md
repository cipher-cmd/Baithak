## What it says
No promoter may advertise, market, book, sell or offer a real estate project for sale without registering it with the state RERA authority (Section 3(1)). Agents who facilitate sales must register with the Authority (Section 9). Promoters must deposit 70 per cent of amounts realised from allottees in a separate account for each project (Section 4(2)(l)(D)). A promoter who breaks Section 3 may be penalised up to 10 per cent of the estimated project cost (Section 59(1)). Continued breach can bring up to three years' imprisonment (Section 59(2)). Each state has its own authority and rules.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app lists, books or takes money for a project that is not registered with the state RERA (Section 3(1)). An agent who facilitates a sale without its own agent registration also breaks Section 9.
- GRAY if: the app shows only projects that carry a RERA number you have checked on the state portal. Whether a listing platform counts as an "agent" under the Act was not settled in the sources reviewed. Section 3(2)(a) exempts projects where the land does not exceed 500 square metres, or the apartments do not exceed eight, so check the state's own threshold.
- Compliant route: show only projects whose RERA number you verified on the state portal. Register the brokerage as an agent in each state where it works.

## Watch for
- The official Act text was not opened. The PDF on indiacode.nic.in returned HTTP 403. The section wording comes from courtbook.in, which reproduces the Act, and from one law-firm page. Verify against the gazette or the state RERA portal before any build decision.
- Section 10 (agent duties) was not verified.
- Section 3(2) thresholds may be changed by state rules. Check the state of each listed project.
- Secondary sources report that some states have diluted the 70 per cent rule.
- Co-living and rental operators: RERA applicability was not checked.
