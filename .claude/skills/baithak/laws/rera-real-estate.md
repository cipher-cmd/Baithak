## What it says
Most new housing projects must register with the state RERA authority before they are advertised or sold. Agents who facilitate sales must register too. Promoters must put 70 per cent of money received from buyers into a separate account for each project. Non-registration can bring a penalty of up to 10 per cent of the estimated project cost (Section 59, as one law-firm note reports). Each state has its own authority and rules.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app lists, books or takes money for a project that is not registered with the state RERA. A promoter who has not registered may not advertise or sell units. An agent who facilitates a sale without its own agent registration also breaks Section 9.
- GRAY if: the app shows only projects that carry a RERA number you have checked on the state portal. Whether a listing platform counts as an "agent" under the Act was not settled in the sources reviewed.
- Compliant route: show only projects whose RERA number you verified on the state portal. Register the brokerage as an agent in each state where it works.

## Watch for
- The official Act text was not opened. indiacode.nic.in returned HTTP 403. The section numbers come from law-firm pages.
- Section 10 (agent duties) and the exact sub-section for the 70 per cent rule were not verified.
- One source gives a 500 sq m exemption. Another mentions an 8-apartment threshold. The second was not verified.
- Secondary sources report that some states have diluted the 70 per cent rule.
- Co-living and rental operators: RERA applicability was not checked.
