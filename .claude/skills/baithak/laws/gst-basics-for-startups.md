## What it says
Services are charged GST at 18% under the post-reform rates. Software and IT services, including SaaS, stayed at 18% after GST 2.0. Registration is required once services turnover passes Rs 20 lakh (Rs 10 lakh in special-category states, per one guide). Foreign providers of online services into India (OIDAR) register from the first transaction. Foreign SaaS bought by Indian businesses is generally taxed under reverse charge. Exports of services can be made without paying GST when the exporter files a Letter of Undertaking (LUT). Sources checked are secondary (guides and vendor sites), not CBIC notifications.

## What it means for a founder
- WHITE for ordinary Indian B2B or B2C SaaS: register once the threshold is crossed, charge 18% on Indian sales, and use the LUT for export sales.
- Day one: a GST number is needed if you sell services above the threshold. Get an accountant to confirm the threshold for your state.
- No legal risk is flagged. The legal exposure is in not registering in time or not charging GST on taxable supplies.

## Watch for
- Confirm the Rs 20 lakh threshold and the exact SAC 9983 treatment on the GST portal or in the CBIC notification (Central Tax (Rate) 9/2025 was cited but not read).
- LUT and e-invoicing thresholds change. Check before filing.
- Verify before each filing. The sources are secondary.
