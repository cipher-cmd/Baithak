## What it says
Four codes replace 29 central labour laws: the Code on Wages, the Industrial Relations Code, the Code on Social Security and the OSH Code. All four came into force on 21 November 2025. For the first time, gig and platform workers are covered by social security provisions. Under section 114, aggregators must contribute 1 to 2 percent of annual turnover to a fund for these workers, capped at 5 percent of what they pay those workers.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: no business model is ruled illegal by these codes in the sources opened.
- GRAY if: you run a gig or platform app. The contribution rate and its start date had not been notified in sources read in September and October 2026. Central rule status is reported inconsistently.
- Compliant route:
  - Employers with 10 or more workers: OSH Code health, safety and welfare duties apply.
  - Aggregators: register the business on the central portal. Register each new gig worker in real time or daily, and share worker details monthly (Rule 48, Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026).
  - Standing orders under the Industrial Relations Code apply from 300 workers (raised from 100).

## Watch for
- Central rules: one source says they are "yet to be notified", another says they were formally notified. Taxmann cites the Social Security (Central) Rules, 2026, G.S.R. 344(E), dated 8 May 2026. Confirm in the Gazette.
- Aggregator contribution: the rate is set by notification under section 114(4) and (5). No notification found.
- ESI and gratuity thresholds were not confirmed. State rules were not checked.
