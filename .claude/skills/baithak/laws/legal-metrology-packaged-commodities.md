## What it says
Packaged goods sold in India must carry declarations on the pack under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. The Amendment Rules, 2026 (G.S.R. 128(E), dated 13 February 2026) insert rule 6(10A). It requires every e-commerce entity selling imported products to show a searchable and sortable filter for country of origin, effective 1 July 2026. A compliance note also cites an earlier e-commerce duty under G.S.R. 629(E), dated 23 June 2017.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: you sell packaged goods through a platform or app without the required declarations on pack or listing, and you know the duty and ignore it. This is not yet tested as a business-model ban.
- GRAY if: you are unsure which declarations apply to your product, or you import goods and are unsure how the country-of-origin filter applies to your listings.
- Compliant route: print the declarations your product category requires on each pack, and on e-commerce listings show country of origin for imported goods from 1 July 2026. Check the current rule text before printing.

## Watch for
- The full list of mandatory pack declarations (rule 6) was not verified. The official text and the Khaitan consolidated PDF could not be read. Do not rely on a list from this file until rule 6 is checked.
- A second amendment, G.S.R. 312(E), dated 27 April 2026, appears in search results with a 1 July 2027 start date. Its text was not opened, so its effect is unverified.
- Penalty amounts under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 were not verified.
