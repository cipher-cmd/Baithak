## What it says
Every food business operator needs an FSSAI licence or registration before it sells food. The category depends on annual turnover. An FSSAI order dated 13 March 2026 sets the current bands: registration up to Rs 1.5 crore, State licence above Rs 1.5 crore up to Rs 50 crore, and Central licence above Rs 50 crore. The bands apply from 1 April 2026 and replace the older thresholds. The order cites the Licensing and Registration Regulations, 2011, as amended by a 2026 amendment gazetted on 10 March 2026.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: you sell food, including home-made or cloud-kitchen food, through any channel without a licence or registration that matches your turnover.
- GRAY if: your turnover sits near a band edge, or you are unsure whether the Central tier applies to your model (for example, multi-state or e-commerce sales). The turnover order does not settle these cases.
- Compliant route: check your expected annual turnover against the three bands, and apply on FoSCoS before you start selling.

## Watch for
- The 2026 order sets turnover bands only. This check did not open the 2011 Regulations text, the kind-of-business eligibility sheets, or the Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations. Labelling and claim rules are not covered here.
- Secondary sources say the Central tier applies to e-commerce and multi-state sellers regardless of turnover. This is not verified against official text. Check FoSCoS before relying on it.
- Sources do not agree on whether home kitchens fall in the basic tier. Verify with FSSAI before advising a home kitchen.
