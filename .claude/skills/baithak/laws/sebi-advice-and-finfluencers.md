## What it says
Under Regulation 3 of the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, no one may act as an investment adviser, or present themselves as one, without a SEBI certificate. Advice for a fee counts. SEBI has treated specific buy or sell recommendations given through social media, messaging or apps as advisory activity that needs registration. SEBI has penalised unregistered advisers in several orders. Since August 2024, SEBI rules bar registered intermediaries (brokers, mutual funds, banks) from working with unregistered finfluencers. A January 2025 circular repeats that bar. Only SEBI-registered advisers may give investment advice.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app or AI gives personalised buy or sell calls on stocks or mutual funds, for a fee or for a subscription, without a SEBI registration. Also BLACK: promoting unregistered advice through a registered broker's or fund's channel.
- GRAY if: the product shows historical data, portfolio analytics or education with no buy or sell recommendation. Get a lawyer's view on the exact wording of any "suggestions".
- Compliant route: partner with a SEBI-registered investment adviser (RIA), research analyst (RA) or broker. Or limit the product to historical data visualisation, with no recommendations.

## Watch for
- Exact text and exemptions of the 2013 Regulations (Regulation 4 lists them; not read in full here).
- SEBI's later circulars on finfluencers and algo trading. The sources point to enforcement and clarifications in 2025, not a new Investment Advisers amendment. Check sebi.gov.in before relying on dates.
- Penalty amounts vary by case. Do not quote a number without checking the order.
