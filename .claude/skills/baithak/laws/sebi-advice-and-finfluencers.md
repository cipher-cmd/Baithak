## What it says
Under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, investment advice needs a SEBI certificate. SEBI has treated specific buy or sell recommendations given through social media, messaging or apps as advice that needs registration. From 29 August 2024, registered intermediaries (exchanges, depositories, mutual funds, investment advisers, brokers) may not have money transactions, client referrals, IT links or joint marketing with unregistered advisers or finfluencers. A circular dated 29 January 2025 reinforces that bar. Stock-market educators may not give investment advice or performance claims unless registered, and may not use live market prices. Breaches can bring fines, or suspension or cancellation of registration.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app or AI gives personalised buy or sell calls on stocks or mutual funds, for a fee or subscription, without SEBI registration. Also BLACK: promoting unregistered advice through a registered broker's or fund's channel.
- GRAY if: the product shows historical data, portfolio analytics or education, with no buy or sell recommendation. Get a lawyer's view on the wording of any "suggestions".
- Compliant route: partner with a SEBI-registered investment adviser, research analyst or broker. Or limit the product to historical data, with no recommendations.

## Watch for
- Official text not opened on 2026-11-10. The 29 January 2025 circular was not found on sebi.gov.in and the NSE copy did not load. Its number is unconfirmed. Check sebi.gov.in before relying on the date or wording.
- The 2013 Regulations were not read in official text. Regulation 3 (certificate rule) and Regulation 4 (exemptions) rest on law-firm summaries.
- One commentator says it is unclear whether some finfluencer arrangements fall under the ban. Get a lawyer's view.
- Penalty amounts vary by case. Do not quote a number without checking the order.
