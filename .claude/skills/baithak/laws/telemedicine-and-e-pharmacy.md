## What it says
**Telemedicine.** Only a registered medical practitioner (RMP), listed on a State or National Medical Register, may give advice or prescribe medicines online. AI and machine-learning platforms may not counsel patients or prescribe. They may support a doctor's evaluation, but the doctor must give the prescription and any counselling. Platforms must check that every doctor they list is registered. A prescription may be sent to a pharmacy only with the patient's consent. Where a physical exam is needed, no online prescription may be issued until one is arranged. Doctors offering online consultation must complete a mandatory course within three years of the guidelines.

**E-pharmacy.** A 2018 draft amendment to the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 would require registration of online pharmacies, bar sales of narcotic and psychotropic drugs, ban advertising of drugs online, and require that data stays in India. The sources checked describe the draft as still not notified as of May 2026. One report mentions a government draft bill on online sale of prescription drugs; its status was not confirmed.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: AI or an app issues or effectively issues a prescription without a registered doctor signing it; or an app lists doctors it has not checked.
- BLACK if: the app sells prescription (Schedule H, H1 or X) medicines online without a licensed pharmacy and a valid prescription process. Get a lawyer's view on the current e-pharmacy position before building.
- GRAY if: the product sells non-prescription items, or the e-pharmacy status is unclear. Also GRAY: a health-data app with no prescribing, which must still meet DPDP rules.
- Compliant route: AI as a clinical assistant for a human RMP, who reviews and signs every prescription. For medicine sales, partner with a licensed pharmacy and follow the current rules.

## Watch for
- The e-pharmacy rules and the draft online-prescription bill. Check the CDSCO and Gazette before any build.
- The guideline issuer. Sources name the NMC or the Board of Governors in supersession of the MCI, so confirm the current body.
- Advertising limits for medicines (Drugs and Magic Remedies Act, 1954) also apply to health apps. Not checked in this file.
