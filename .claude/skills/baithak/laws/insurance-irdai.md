## What it says
A web aggregator is an IRDAI-registered intermediary that runs a website to compare insurance products. It registers on Form A with a non-refundable fee of Rs 10,000. An applicant cannot already be registered as an insurance agent, corporate agent, micro-insurance agent, TPA, surveyor, insurance marketing firm or other intermediary (regulation 3(a)(iv)). It cannot have a referral arrangement with an insurer (regulation 3(a)(v)). The 2025 Amendment Act (Sabka Bima Sabki Raksha) widened the intermediary regime.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app sells, solicits or telemarkets insurance without the right IRDAI registration. Also if a registered aggregator has a referral deal with an insurer, or its telemarketing staff are untrained or unexamined.
- GRAY if: the app only shows product details with no sale or solicitation. Embedded insurance was not covered by any source opened, so it is unclear.
- Compliant route:
  - Comparison site: register as a web aggregator (Form A). List the insurers you deal with (regulation 21(a)). Do not push one company's product (regulation 21(c)). Base the product on a need analysis (regulation 21(b)).
  - Selling or servicing: partner with a registered corporate agent or insurance marketing firm, or obtain that registration yourself.
  - Telemarketing: use only trained and examined staff. They may sell only by telemarketing and must not make unsolicited outbound calls (Form O).
