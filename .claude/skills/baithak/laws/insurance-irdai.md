## What it says
A web aggregator is an IRDAI-registered intermediary that runs a website comparing insurance products. It applies on Form A with a non-refundable Rs 10,000 fee. An applicant cannot already be registered as an insurance agent, corporate agent, TPA, surveyor, insurance marketing firm or other intermediary (regulation 3(a)(iv), per a legal-database copy). It cannot have a referral arrangement with an insurer (regulation 3(a)(v)). IRDAI's FAQ says aggregators may not use agents or point-of-sale persons to solicit, and may market only products listed in Form Q.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the app sells, solicits or telemarkets insurance without the right IRDAI registration. Also if it uses agents to solicit, or markets products outside Form Q.
- GRAY if: the app only shows product details with no sale or solicitation. Insurer lead deals are unclear: the FAQ allows leads under a written agreement, while regulation 3(a)(v) bars referral arrangements. Get legal advice first.
- Compliant route:
  - Comparison site: register as a web aggregator (Form A). Do not push one insurer's product (regulation 21(c)). With several insurer tie-ups, list them for the customer (regulation 21(a)). Base the product on a need analysis (regulation 21(b)).
  - Selling or servicing: partner with a registered corporate agent or insurance marketing firm, or get that registration.
