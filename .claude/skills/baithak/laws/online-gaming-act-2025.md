## What it says
The Act received presidential assent on 22 August 2025. It bans "online money games" nationwide: any online game where users deposit money expecting a money return. The ban covers games of skill, games of chance, and mixes of both. Banks and payment providers may not process payments for them, and advertising them is prohibited. E-sports and free social or casual games are carved out and promoted. The Act's start date is set by Gazette notification (section 1(3)). MeitY notified the commencement and the Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Rules, 2026 on 22 April 2026. Both took effect on 1 May 2026. The Online Gaming Authority of India, an attached office of MeitY, enforces it.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: users pay money to play for a money or money-equivalent prize. This includes fantasy sports, rummy, poker, and opinion or prediction trading built as a money game. It also covers promoting such games or processing their payments.
- GRAY if: the mechanic sits on the edge, such as paid entry with non-cash prizes, or rewards convertible to cash. Get a lawyer's view on the Rules before launch.
- Compliant route: free-to-play with ads, subscriptions or sponsorships; e-sports; skill games with no money stake.

## Watch for
- The PIB and MeitY pages returned HTTP 403 today. The commencement and Rules dates above come from law-firm and news sources. Check the Gazette notification before relying on them.
- Penalties conflict between sources. A press report gives up to 3 years' jail and Rs 1 crore in fines. A PIB backgrounder gives different figures for repeat offences. Check the Act text.
- Registration applies only to notified games and all e-sports offerings.
