## What it says
The Act got presidential assent on 22 August 2025. It bans "online money games" nationwide: any online game where users deposit money expecting a money return. The ban covers games of skill, games of chance, and mixes of both. Banks and payment providers may not process payments for them, and advertising them is prohibited. E-sports and free social or casual games are carved out and promoted. Reports put the ban's start date around 1 October 2025. MeitY published draft rules in October 2025.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: users pay money to play for a money or money-equivalent prize. This includes fantasy sports, rummy, poker, and opinion or prediction trading built as a money game. It also covers promoting such games or processing their payments.
- GRAY if: the mechanic sits on the edge, such as paid entry with non-cash prizes, or rewards convertible to cash. Wait for the final rules and get a lawyer's view.
- Compliant route: free-to-play with ads, subscriptions or sponsorships; e-sports; skill games with no money stake.

## Watch for
The final rules, the authority the Act sets up, and any court challenge. Several operators (for example WinZO and PokerBaazi) paused real-money products after the Act.
