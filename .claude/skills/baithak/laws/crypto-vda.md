## What it says
Crypto is not banned in India, and there is no comprehensive crypto law. Two rules bite in practice.

1. **Tax deduction at source.** Section 194S (in force since 1 July 2022) requires a 1% TDS on the value of a transfer of a virtual digital asset (VDA) by an Indian resident. The threshold depends on the payer: Rs 50,000 for a "specified person" and Rs 10,000 for others. Tax is filed on Form 26QE. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026, and the section number may have changed. Sources disagree, so check the new Act's numbering.
2. **Anti-money-laundering registration.** Crypto exchanges and similar businesses must register with FIU-IND under the PMLA framework, and comply with its reporting rules. Sources describe this as the core compliance signal. No 2026 official notice changing the rules was found.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: the business runs an exchange, a wallet with transfers, or a trading service for VDAs without FIU-IND registration; or it skips the 1% TDS on transfers it facilitates.
- GRAY if: the product only shows crypto prices, tracks a user's tax, or helps with TDS filing, with no custody or transfers. The rules for these are not fully settled.
- Compliant route: register with FIU-IND, deduct the 1% TDS automatically, and run KYC and transaction reporting. Or stay out of custody and transfers.

## Watch for
- The section number and wording under the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- Any policy paper or new law on crypto. Sources in this check reported none finalised as of 2026.
- Official FIU-IND and Income Tax Department notices were not opened. Verify before relying on any threshold.
