## What it says
Plants that may pollute need the State Pollution Control Board's prior consent to establish, under the Water Act, 1974 and the Air Act, 1981. White category plants are exempt if they inform the board in writing. Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) requires producers, importers and brand owners (PIBOs) to register with CPCB. E-waste producers must register on a CPCB portal and may not deal with unregistered entities. Every battery manufacturer must register as a producer.

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: you build a non-white plant without the state board's consent. Or you import or sell plastic packaging, e-waste or batteries without EPR registration. Registered producers may deal only with registered brand owners.
- GRAY if: you are not sure your plant is white, or which EPR roles you hold. One company can hold several roles, each with its own registration. Battery importers are unclear.
- Compliant route: confirm your category with the state board before you build. Register on the CPCB EPR portal for each role before you import or sell.
