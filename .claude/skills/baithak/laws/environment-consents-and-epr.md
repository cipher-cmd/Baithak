## What it says
Plants that may pollute need the State Pollution Control Board's prior consent to establish, under the Water Act, 1974 and the Air Act, 1981. Notifications dated 12 November 2024 exempt white category plants from consent to establish and operate. They must inform the State board in writing. Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) requires producers, importers and brand owners (PIBOs) to register under the Plastic Waste Management Rules. E-waste producers and recyclers must register on a CPCB portal, and the 2022 Rules bar them from operating or dealing with unregistered entities. Battery producers and manufacturers must register with CPCB (Rule 4(4), Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022).

## What it means for a founder
- BLACK if: you build a non-white plant without the state board's consent. Or you handle e-waste without CPCB registration, or handle plastic packaging as a producer or brand owner without the registration the PWM Rules require.
- GRAY if: you are not sure your plant is white, or which EPR roles you hold. One company can hold several roles, each with its own registration. Battery importers: the sources reviewed do not say whether registration must come before import or sale.
- Compliant route: confirm your category with the state board before you build. Register on the CPCB EPR portal for each role before you import or sell, where the rules require it.

## Watch for
- Official notification text was not opened. The Parliament answer on sansad.in returned a connection error. Sources disagree on the number: one law-firm copy cites G.S.R. 702(E) while its text is 703(E). Sources also disagree on which Act each uses.
- CPCB pages list notice titles and dates only, not the operative text. CPCB's site lists a single sign-on notice for existing EPR portals (updated 13 February 2026). Portal addresses in secondary sources conflict.
- A law-firm update reports a CPCB public notice dated 27 August 2026 on registration for entities under the PWM Rules. Not opened.
- The "no dealing with unregistered entities" e-waste bar rests on one opened law-firm reading. The Assam PCB SOP could not be read.
- The rule that registered plastic producers may deal only with registered brand owners was not confirmed.
- Penalty figures and the Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2026 were not verified.
