## What it says
The E-Commerce Rules, 2020 apply to marketplaces and sellers. Rule 4(9) requires explicit, affirmative consumer consent to a purchase. Pre-ticked boxes are not allowed. Platforms must also meet duties on product quality, delivery and grievance redressal. The Dark Patterns Guidelines (30 November 2023) name 13 specified dark patterns, such as false urgency, basket sneaking and subscription traps, and may be extended. The CCPA said in June 2025 that all e-commerce platforms should self-audit for dark patterns within three months and file self-declarations. Several sources describe that as an advisory, and the Department has issued notices to 11 platforms under the Guidelines. Sources differ on whether the Guidelines are binding.

## What it means for a founder
- Treat as BLACK if: a checkout or subscription uses any of the 13 named dark patterns (for example, fake countdown timers, hidden cancellation, pre-ticked add-ons, or basket sneaking). Notices have already been issued under the Guidelines.
- GRAY if: the product is a marketplace or D2C store and the dark-pattern position is not yet settled on every point. Build to the Guidelines anyway.
- Compliant route: explicit opt-in for every add-on; a cancel button that is as easy as signing up; honest urgency claims; a self-audit log.

## Watch for
- The text of the 2023 Guidelines and the list of dark patterns. Check the CCPA website before relying on any item.
- Whether the Guidelines are binding in every case. Sources differ.
- Any further CCPA notices. This check did not find the 2026 status.
