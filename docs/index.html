02The method

          
Idea in. India check. Clear call out.

        

        
Not a score from a generic prompt. The Chair states its assumptions and runs the checks before any advisor speaks.

      

      

        
01
The Chair checks India
Who pays, what kind of business it is, where it gets stuck, what breaks at scale, and the legal zone.

        
02
You choose the council
The recommended board, the seats you pick, one advisor alone, or all five.

        
03
They argue it out
Opening views, a debate, revised positions. Every round is saved.

        
04
You get a decision
A scored verdict, the strongest objection, a 7-day test under ₹5,000 and a point to walk away.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        

          
03Before the debate

          
First, the Chair checks India.

        

        
The checks US validators skip. In India they decide most outcomes. Watch them tick.

      

      

        
01 / Customer
Who actually pays?
India 1, India 2, India 3 or India-to-Global. Mixing them is a failure on its own.
India 1India 2India 3Global

          

        
05 / Legal zone
WHITE, GRAY, BLACK or UNMAPPED
Cited only from 24 dated, sourced law files.

          
Known path, buildWHITE
Build with a compliance switchGRAY
Vetoed, find the legal routeBLACK
Not covered, ask the regulatorUNMAPPED

        
02 / Business model
What kind of business?
A pickle brand is not judged like a SaaS tool.

        
03 / Friction
Where does it get stuck?
The ₹12k employee test, the CA gatekeeper, UPI collection, public rails like ONDC.

        
04 / Scale
What breaks at 10,000?
The metro bubble, USD costs on INR revenue, human ops, pin-code and language walls.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        

          
04The playbook

          
Eight idea types. Different tests.

        

        
One checklist does not fit every business. Regulated sectors get an extra legal bar on top of their type.

      

      

        
TYPE 01
Software and SaaS
Margins after cloud and AI, payback, global pricing.

        
TYPE 02
Consumer app and content
Habit, retention, revenue per user.

        
TYPE 03
Marketplace
Liquidity, off-platform leakage, take rate.

        
TYPE 04
Physical product and D2C
Margins, channel stack, working capital, COD returns.

        
TYPE 05
Manufacturing and hardware
Capex, utilisation, import dependence, certification time.

        
TYPE 06
Service business
Founder dependence, utilisation, productisation.

        
TYPE 07
Local business
Rent share, break-even orders, commissions.

        
TYPE 08
Regulated sector
Fintech, health, education, real estate, insurance. Stricter legal checks on top.

The call: don't build the paid-entry app; it's an online money game and is vetoed. Build nothing yet. Run a no-code WhatsApp college league and pitch sponsors; reopen the free version only if the gates are met.

          
A 1.3 · B 4.1 · C 5.3 · legal zone A BLACK, B GRAY

08Evidence, not theatre

          
Every claim has a trail.

        

        
Baithak shows what it knows, what it doesn't, and what still needs checking.

      

      

        
24
Dated law files
Each lists its sources, a last-verified date and what to watch for.

        
1,191
Sourced quotes
Verbatim lines across five brains, each tied to a source id.

        
54
Public sources
Interviews, podcasts, talks and letters behind the five seats.

        
0
Quiet WHITEs
Anything outside coverage returns UNMAPPED, never a quiet pass.

      

      

        
Not legal or financial adviceGRAY and BLACK always end with "verify with a lawyer before you build".

        
Sources have limitsEach law file records what is still unverified against official text.

        
Open source · MITA fork of Roast Room, rebuilt for India.

      

    

  


  

    

      

        

          
09Get started

          
Pull up a chair.

        

        
Runs inside Claude Code. Your Claude plan covers it. No API key, no signup.

      

      

        

          
Recommended · plugin

          
Two commands

          
Inside Claude Code. The skill is then /baithak:baithak.

          
/plugin marketplace add cipher-cmd/Baithak

          
/plugin install baithak@baithak

        

        

          
Any project · skills CLI

          
One command

          
In your project folder, then start with --quick.

          
npx skills add cipher-cmd/Baithak

          
/baithak --quick I want to build …

        

        

          
Build on it · clone

          
Make it yours

          
Build new seats or edit law files.

          
git clone https://github.com/cipher-cmd/Baithak.git

          
cd Baithak && claude

        

      

      

        
Start small--quick seats four advisors for two rounds. A full five-seat council is fifteen runs.

        
Choose the roomThe recommended board, the seats you tick, one advisor, or everyone.

        
Keep the recordBrief, checks, every round and the verdict are saved in sessions/.
