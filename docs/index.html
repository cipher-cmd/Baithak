First, the Chair checks India.

    
US validators skip this part. Here it decides most outcomes.

    

      

        
Who actually pays
India 1, India 2, India 3 or India-to-Global. Mixing them is a failure on its own.

        
What kind of business
Eight types, each with its own tests. A pickle brand is not judged like a SaaS tool.

        
Where it gets stuck
The ₹12k employee test, the CA gatekeeper, UPI collection, public rails like ONDC.

        
What breaks at scale
The metro bubble, USD costs on INR revenue, human ops, pin-code and language walls.
