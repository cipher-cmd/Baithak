Baithak · The Indian Council

Bring an idea to Baithak.

A council of AI advisors for people building in India. It checks who pays, what breaks, and whether it is even legal, then argues it out and hands you a scored verdict, a 7-day test under ₹5,000, and the point to walk away.

US validators don't know India. This one does.

Before a single advisor speaks, the Chair runs the checks that decide most Indian startups. Then the council argues, changes its mind for real reasons, and votes.

01 · BRIEF

Describe the idea

One line, who pays, what they do today, your budget and your lean. Say "just run it" and the Chair fills gaps with stated assumptions.

02 · INDIA CHECKS

The Chair checks India first

India 1, 2, 3 or global. Eight idea types, each with its own tests. Friction, scalability walls, and a WHITE, GRAY or BLACK legal zone.

03 · VERDICT

The council argues and scores

Advisors debate over two or three rounds. You get the call, the strongest objection, a 7-day jugaad test and kill criteria.

Install in a minute

Runs inside Claude Code. Your Claude plan covers it; no API key, no signup.

As a Claude Code plugin (recommended), inside Claude Code:

/plugin marketplace add cipher-cmd/Baithak
/plugin install baithak@baithak

Or with the skills CLI, in your project folder:

npx skills add cipher-cmd/Baithak

Then bring your idea. Start with --quick: four advisors, two rounds.

/baithak --quick I want to build …

Honest about its limits

  • Not legal or financial advice. The legal zone tells you what to check. GRAY and BLACK always end with "verify with a lawyer".
  • Law files are dated and sourced. Several rest on law-firm summaries because official sites block automated reading; each file says what is unverified.
  • Advisors are simulations built from public statements, not the people, and are not endorsed by them. No faces, no voices. Anyone named can opt out.
  • Open source (MIT). CAs, lawyers and founders: the law files and advisors get better with your pull requests.
